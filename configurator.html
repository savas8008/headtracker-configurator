How do I find my reset AUX channel?

        

    
ELRS numbers channels as AUX, with an offset
    (AUX1 = CH5). Where head tracking data actually goes depends entirely
    on HT Start Channel — if Aux… is selected it overrides three RC channels,
    if EdgeTX is selected it goes to the radio as a trainer input without touching
    any channel. Choose your mode and the channel you're considering below.
